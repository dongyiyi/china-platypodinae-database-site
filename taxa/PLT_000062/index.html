Checklist status: Unreviewed. Primary distribution evidence is available, but formal acceptance has not been assigned.

Distribution

China — Yunnan (Xishuangbanna; literature-reported, pending checklist acceptance). Lai et al. (2020) reported six specimens, of which five were obtained from rubber-tree log dissection and one from an ethanol-baited trap. Source: Lai et al. (2020).

Original type locality — Thailand, Donglam. The original type series was collected in October 1963. This is not a Chinese distribution record. Source: Browne (1972).

Hosts

Afzelia xylocarpa — Thailand (Donglam), original type specimens described as collected ex this plant. Source: Browne (1972).

Hevea brasiliensis — China (Yunnan), five adults recovered by dissection of rubber-tree logs. This is a wood/substrate association, not evidence of attacks on healthy standing trees. Source: Lai et al. (2020).

The ethanol-trapped specimen is not assigned a plant host. Countries and methods are retained from the respective source.

Taxonomy & nomenclature

The original and currently used combinations are identical; no verified additional synonym or replacement name is linked to this taxon.

2020 · Subsequent published usage

Platypus afzeliae Browne, 1972. Reported in a Yunnan beetle survey. This does not constitute a new combination or synonymy. Source: Lai et al. (2020).

1972 · Original description

Platypus afzeliae Browne, 1972, pp. 29–30. Original Thai type series recorded from Afzelia xylocarpa. Source: Browne (1972).

Data resources

Specimens, images and molecular information

Type specimens are documented by Browne (1972); six Chinese specimens are reported by Lai et al. (2020). No public reuse permission for images or verified molecular accession is established by this pilot.
